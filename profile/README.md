### What we make

- **Control4 drivers** – free and Pro, built to go further than the drivers they replace
- **Gorse Bridge** – one hub that links control systems to the services and devices they can't reach on their own (on site, in the cloud, or both)
- **Gorse Labs Studio** – online tools for installers working with Loxone, KNX, Control4 and Home Assistant
- **Templates** – ready-made device templates, starting with Loxone ([browse them here](https://github.com/Gorse-Labs/templates))
- **Custom work** – drivers, software and hardware for jobs nothing off the shelf can handle

### Platforms

Control4 · Loxone · KNX · Nice ELAN · Home Assistant

### Why "Gorse Labs"?

Gorse is the tough New Forest shrub that flowers almost all year round, hardy like our drivers.
"Labs" is the workshop, where things get taken apart, tested, and the "impossible" ideas get tried.
